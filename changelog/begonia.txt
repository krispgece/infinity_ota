Build 24.09.2026 | Security patch 2026-09-01 | Kernel 4.14.357

Fixes from a full test pass of the first release.

• VoWiFi (Wi-Fi calling) works. Part of MediaTek's VoWiFi stack was missing from the vendor blobs, and Android 16 blocked a routing rule the IMS IPsec monitor needs. Both are fixed. In testing, a call moved from Wi-Fi to LTE without dropping. Turn it on under SIM settings > Wi-Fi calling.

• Wi-Fi hotspot turns on at the first tap while the phone is connected to a 5 GHz network, and runs on 5 GHz. The Wi-Fi driver marked channels 36-48 as radar channels, so the hotspot refused to start on them.

• Off-mode charging works. With a charger connected, powering off shows the charging screen instead of starting Android again.

Known issues:

• Video calling over the carrier network (ViLTE / ViWiFi) is not available. Video calls in apps such as WhatsApp work.

• Please report anything else you find, with a logcat if you can.
